📱 Install on your phone

ऐप फ़ोन में लगाइए — बिना डाउनलोड

Sankirtan is a web app — nothing to download from a store. Open sankirtan.app/install, and the page shows the exact steps for your phone. On Android it's one tap; on iPhone it's Share → Add to Home Screen.

Once installed it opens like any app, works offline for bhajans you've already opened, and updates itself. When a new version is ready, a small banner asks you to refresh.

🔍 Finding a bhajan

भजन खोजिए — हिंदी या अंग्रेज़ी में
  • Type in either script. babosa and बाबोसा both find the same bhajan. Title, lyrics and तर्ज़ are all searched.
  • Speak it. Tap the 🎤 in the search box. The small chip next to it switches voice between हिंदी and English.
  • Filter by Deity, Type (bhajan, aarti, chalisa…) or Mood (#diwali, #morning, #dance…).
  • Discover. Tap 🔥 Popular Bhajans or ✨ Recently Added to see eight picks. Tap again to close.
  • Compact / Full. Compact shows more titles per screen; Full shows a lyric preview and moods.

📖 Reading a bhajan

भजन पढ़िए — बड़े अक्षर, तर्ज़ और मूड के साथ
  • तर्ज़ (the tune it's sung to) sits under the title when known.
  • Text size — use the reading settings (Aa) to make lyrics larger or switch to dark mode for night jagrans.
  • Swipe left or right to move to the next or previous bhajan in the list you came from.
  • Moods appear as chips at the bottom; Related bhajans below them share the same deity or moods.
  • ↗ Share sends a link to WhatsApp. 🔗 Source opens where the lyrics came from.
  • 📋 Add to puts the bhajan straight into a playlist, program or medley. If it isn't in your library yet, it's saved for you first.

📚 My Library

आपका अपना संग्रह

Signing in (free, with Google) unlocks a personal library. Tap 💾 Save on any public bhajan to copy it there. Your copy is yours: edit the lyrics your way, fix a line, add notes, change the तर्ज़ — the public version stays untouched.

Everything you save can go into playlists, programs and medleys. Your library and lists sync across your phone, tablet and laptop.

Guests can browse and read everything. Saving, playlists and editing need a sign-in.

➕ Adding your own bhajan

नया भजन जोड़िए — रोमन में टाइप करें, देवनागरी में पाएँ
  1. My Library → + Bhajan.
  2. Turn on 🇮🇳 हिंदी ON. Type in English letters — shyam becomes श्याम. Press space or tap a suggestion.
  3. Fill Deity (who it's about), Type (bhajan / aarti / chalisa), Moods (#diwali, #morning…) and तर्ज़ if you know the tune.
  4. Or tap 📥 Auto-fill from a photo to read lyrics from a picture of a bhajan book — the text is read on your phone, nothing is uploaded.
Leave a blank line after the mukhda. The mukhda is the opening refrain. A blank line before the first verse tells the app where it ends — that's what Medley view uses. A small chip under the lyrics box confirms: Mukhda: 2 lines.

🎵 Playlists & 📅 Programs

रोज़ के लिए प्लेलिस्ट, जगरण के लिए प्रोग्राम

Both are ordered lists of bhajans from your library. The difference is only when you use them:

  • Playlist — no date. "Morning prayers", "Babosa favourites", "Kids' set".
  • Program — a dated event with a venue. "Diwali Jagran, 30 Oct, Community Hall". Programs have Live mode.

Create: Playlists tab → + Create → pick the type → name it → add bhajans from the picker. Save is at the top.

Edit later: open the list → Edit. Adding, removing or reordering saves automatically — you'll see ✓ Setlist saved. Only a name or date change needs Save details.

From a bhajan: tap 📋 Add to while reading any bhajan, choose the list, done.

🎭 Medleys

मेडली — सिर्फ़ मुखड़े, एक के बाद एक

A medley is for singing several short bhajans back to back. Instead of full lyrics it shows only the mukhda of each bhajan, one after another, so you can see the whole sequence on one screen. Tap any mukhda for the full lyrics; the back button returns you to the medley.

Medleys live in My Library (they're something you sing, like a bhajan). Create one with + 🎭 Medley, pick 4–8 bhajans, name it. Each mukhda also shows its तर्ज़ so you know the tune to switch to.

Medley view depends on the blank-line rule above. If a bhajan shows too many or too few lines, open it in your library and add a blank line after the mukhda.

🎤 Live mode

मंच के लिए — बड़े अक्षर, पूरी स्क्रीन

Open a Program and tap START LIVE PERFORMANCE. Lyrics fill the screen in large text, one bhajan at a time, in your setlist order. Swipe or use the arrows to move; tap Aa to go bigger. Keep the screen awake in your phone's settings for long jagrans.

Live mode is for Programs. To perform a playlist live, Edit it and switch its type to Program.

🌟 Bhajan of the day

आज का भजन

A small card at the top of the Public Library shows one chosen bhajan each day, sometimes with a note on why. It's a good way to learn something new — tap it to read.

💡 Tips for singers

कुछ सुझाव
  • Before a jagran, build the Program the day before and open it once on Wi-Fi — every bhajan in it is then available offline.
  • Add the तर्ज़ to bhajans you save. It shows in setlists and medleys, right when you need it.
  • Use Moods for how you actually pick songs: #fast, #slow, #dance, #opening, #closing.
  • Keep one Playlist called "Learning" for bhajans you're still memorising.
  • Found a mistake in a public bhajan? Save it and fix your copy — and tell us so the public one can be corrected.
