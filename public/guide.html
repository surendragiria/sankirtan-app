📱 Install on your phone

ऐप फ़ोन में लगाइए
  • Android: install Sankirtan from the Play Store, or open sankirtan.app in Chrome and tap Install app.
  • iPhone: open sankirtan.app in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Use the home-screen icon from then on — it keeps your library and recordings safe and works offline.

Sign in with Google (one tap) to get your own library, playlists and programs on every device. The app updates itself; a small banner tells you when a new version is ready.

🔍 Finding a bhajan

भजन खोजिए — हिंदी या अंग्रेज़ी में
  • Search at the top of the Library in either script — babosa and बाबोसा find the same bhajan. Spelling needn't be exact; suggestions appear as you type. Tap 🎤 to speak instead (हिं/EN switches the voice language).
  • Filter with the chips under the search box: ⭐ Starred, Deity ▾, Type ▾ (bhajan, aarti, chalisa…), and moods (#marwari, #bhawna, #dance, #melody — More for the rest).
  • Sort (⇅): A–Z, recently opened, most sung, or recently added. The same menu switches between compact rows and full cards.
  • Continue — the row above the list shows the bhajans you opened last, one tap away.
  • Long-press any bhajan in the list for a quick menu: star, add to a playlist, share, edit. Swipe right to star, swipe left to add to a list.

📖 Reading a bhajan

भजन पढ़िए — बड़े अक्षर, तर्ज़, स्केल और नोट्स
  • तर्ज़ (tune) and scale (C#, D…) show under the title when known. 📝 keeps your private notes for that bhajan — starting sur, who sings which verse.
  • Text size, line spacing and dark mode are in the reading settings (Aa). The screen stays on while a bhajan is open.
  • ▶ Auto-scroll moves the lyrics at your pace — slow it right down for alaap. Touching the lyrics pauses it.
  • ▶ Listen plays the YouTube reference inside the app, where one has been added.
  • Swipe left/right for the next or previous bhajan in the list you came from. Related bhajans (same mood, then same deity) are listed below.
  • ⭐ stars it, 📋 adds it to a playlist, program or medley, ↗ shares the lyrics on WhatsApp.

Every public bhajan is already in your library — no saving needed. Edit one and your copy becomes yours; the public version stays untouched.

➕ Adding your own bhajan

नया भजन जोड़िए — फ़ोटो से, या रोमन में टाइप करके
  1. Library → round + button → Bhajan.
  2. From a photo or PDF: tap Camera, Gallery or Document under Upload. The lyrics are read for you — printed pages or handwriting — in a few seconds. Check the text before saving.
  3. By typing: with हिं on, type in English letters — shyam becomes श्याम on space. Tap a grey chip to keep a word in English. ⛶ opens a full-screen editor; 🧹 Clean up removes stray timestamps and spaces; ↶ undoes.
  4. Set Deity and Type; add the YouTube link, तर्ज़, scale and moods if you know them.
Leave a blank line after the mukhda. The mukhda is the opening refrain; the blank line tells the app where it ends (medleys and the mukhda highlight depend on it). A chip under the lyrics confirms it.

🎵 Playlists, 📅 Programs & 🎭 Medleys

रोज़ के लिए प्लेलिस्ट, जगरण के लिए प्रोग्राम, मुखड़ों के लिए मेडली
  • Playlist — an undated set: "Morning riyaaz", "Babosa favourites".
  • Program — a dated event with a venue and, if you like, a singer assigned to each bhajan: "Diwali Jagran · 30 Oct · Community Hall".
  • Medley — only the mukhda of each bhajan, one after another, for singing several short pieces back to back. Tap a mukhda for the full lyrics.

Create: Playlists or Programs tab → + Create (medleys: Playlists tab → + Medley, or the Library's + button). Adding, removing and reordering bhajans saves automatically.

Share or print: on a program, the ⋯ menu shares the setlist as text on WhatsApp, prints it (with or without lyrics), or duplicates it for the next event.

🎤 Live mode

मंच के लिए — बड़े अक्षर, पूरी स्क्रीन, हाथ खाली

Open a program (or playlist) and tap Start live performance. One bhajan at a time fills the screen in large text, in setlist order:

  • A− / A+ text size · ▶ auto-scroll with speed control · screen never switches off.
  • Swipe or use the arrows for the next bhajan; the next title is shown so you can announce it.
  • The mukhda is highlighted, and your scale and notes are shown for each bhajan.

🎙 Recording your singing

अपना गायन रिकॉर्ड करें — फ़ीडबैक के लिए
  • Tap 🎙 on a bhajan page or in Live mode to start; it shows a red timer. Tap again to stop and save.
  • In Live mode each bhajan is saved as its own recording automatically when you move to the next one.
  • Recordings appear under the bhajan and under the program: ▶ to listen, Share to send on WhatsApp for feedback, 🗑 to delete.
Recordings stay on your phone only — nothing is uploaded. On iPhone, use the home-screen app (not Safari) so they're never cleared.

📶 Offline & low network

बिना नेटवर्क के भी

Once you've opened the app on Wi-Fi or 4G, the whole library, your playlists and programs are kept on the phone. In a hall with no signal the app opens instantly and everything reads normally; edits you make are sent when the network returns.

💡 Tips for singers

कुछ सुझाव
  • Build the Program the day before and open it once on Wi-Fi.
  • Add the scale and a short note to bhajans you perform — they show right where you need them, in Live mode.
  • Record a rehearsal and listen back once before the event.
  • Keep a "Learning" Playlist for bhajans you're still memorising.
  • Spotted a mistake in a public bhajan? Use the in-app Feedback (menu) so it can be corrected for everyone.
