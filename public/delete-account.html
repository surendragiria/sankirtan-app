Delete Your Account

Sankirtan — Bhajan to Bhagwan · Developer: Surendra Kumar Jain

You can request deletion of your Sankirtan account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion is free, permanent, and does not require you to give a reason.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to surendrajain@aol.in from the Google account you use to sign in to Sankirtan.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Sankirtan account".
  3. We will confirm your request by reply and delete your account within 30 days.
Email a deletion request
Don't want to delete everything? You can remove individual bhajans, playlists, medleys or programs from inside the app at any time — open the item and tap Delete. You can also simply sign out; your data stays untouched until you choose to delete it.

What gets deleted

DataAction
Your account record (Google display name, email, profile photo URL)Deleted
Your personal bhajan library, including bhajans you added or editedDeleted
Your playlists, medleys and programs (setlists)Deleted
Keywords, tags and venue names you enteredDeleted
Feedback you submitted through the appDeleted
Cached data in your browser or deviceNot held by us — clear it from your browser or uninstall the app

What is kept

  • Public bhajans you contributed to the shared library are retained, with your name removed, so that other devotees can continue to use them. If you would like a specific public contribution removed as well, say so in your email and we will remove it.
  • Aggregated, anonymous analytics (for example, total daily users) cannot be traced to you and are retained.
  • No other data is kept. There is no retention period beyond the 30-day processing window.

Questions

Email surendrajain@aol.in. Our full Privacy Policy describes everything we collect and why.
